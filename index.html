P4 Nästa Stjärna is Sveriges Radio's nationwide competition for new music and new talent. Every local P4 station holds its own final and sends one act on to the national final. Friends Forever — the first song Sharpe & Jones ever wrote — is in the Västerbotten final, live on stage at Väven in Umeå on Tuesday 20 October.

Listeners help decide who goes through. Voting details and the link will be posted here the moment they're announced.

The duo

The voice and the sound.

Sharpe & Jones are Elvin Sharpe and Morris Jones. They started writing together in November 2025 and haven't stopped since: two singles so far, all of it co-written, with Elvin out front on vocals and Morris shaping the production.

What one starts, the other finishes. Elvin paints the broad strokes; Morris fine-tunes them until they're right. Everything the duo releases passes through both of them.

Vocals

Elvin Sharpe

Elvin Schäder

The voice of the duo. Elvin has played guitar for years and picks up whatever a song needs, both when it's written and when it's recorded. Vulnerable but sure of himself, he writes to make sense of things — and to help others do the same.
